Settings

Tell the app where to find your phones.

Phone network

Phone addresses can change. The app scans these subnets every five minutes and follows enrolled phones by their certificate and MAC address.

One private IPv4 subnet per line, /24 or smaller. Maximum 1,024 addresses.

Phone administrator access

These credentials are used only for phones you enroll. They are separate from this app’s sign-in password.

Saved credentials are encrypted in the persistent data volume.
